About Me

I am a multimedia artist with years of experience with video editing and filmmaking. I'm currently a sophomore at Far Eastern University Institute of Technology, taking Bachelor of Multimedia Arts. I have so far worked on various projects, including some 3 short films, event videography, and game level designing.

My journey as a multimedia artist began in second-year high school, where my friend asked me if I would be interested to write a script for a fan-made Spider-Man film. I accepted the offer, and though we only managed to partially finish the script, that experience sparked my love for the art of filmmaking and storytelling. Since then, I have been honing my skills in video editing, sound design, and ability to create films.

Although me and my friend's paths diverged as the years went on, we still frequently collaborate on projects, even resulting in a short film that we submitted to the 2025 Cinegoma Film Festival. Now, while also balancing my studies, I strive to continue creating all sorts of projects, no matter how big or small.

My Education

Manila Cathedral School (2019 - 2024)

I studied at Manila Cathedral School for my entire high school education, graduating in 2024 under the STEM strand. During my time there, I was a member of the school's multimedia club, Liksari. There, we would be the ones appointed to cover school events through photography and videography. I was one of the pioneers of the club, with the club being established in 2022. I served as a videographer and video editor for the club.

FEU Institute of Technology (2024 - Present)

Currently pursuing a Bachelor of Multimedia Arts degree, I am now a sophomore. My time here at FEU, I have yet to join any clubs or organizations, but has been my most fruitful time in terms of being able to create projects such as short films. As I have been able to work on 3 short films in my first year.
